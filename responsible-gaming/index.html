2. State Legal Restrictions in India

Real-money card and skill gaming operations are subject to regional statutory regulations across India. In accordance with applicable state laws, users located in regions where real-money skill gaming is legally restricted or prohibited (including, but not limited to, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Odisha, Sikkim, and Nagaland) must refrain from downloading, depositing, or participating in these platforms. It is the player's responsibility to verify local compliance before engaging in any online gaming activity.

3. Principles of Healthy & Responsible Play

  • Set Strict Limits: Establish non-negotiable time and budget boundaries before launching any application.
  • Avoid Loss Chasing: Never deposit additional money in an attempt to recover prior losses.
  • Entertainment Only: Treat gaming purely as recreational leisure, never as a guaranteed income stream or investment scheme.
  • Balance Your Time: Ensure online gaming does not interfere with personal, family, or work responsibilities.
